Private collection. Independent recovery.

A safe way back to your collection.

J-Collection Recovery is the backup and recovery component of J-Collection, a personal application that manages a collection on its owner's computer.

What it does

It creates encrypted recovery copies of the local catalog, personal notes and ratings, reviewed artwork originals, and application settings. Recovery previews let the owner compare saved decisions with current ones before applying changes.

Recovery archives exclude collection video files, OAuth credentials, and the recovery encryption key.

Why it connects to Google Drive

With the owner's consent, it stores encrypted backups in the owner's selected Google Drive account. It downloads backups to verify they can be decrypted and validated, and removes older backups according to the configured retention policy after a replacement has been verified.

It requests the limited drive.file permission to manage files created through this OAuth application or explicitly made available to it. It also checks the connected account's email address and available Drive storage to confirm the destination before uploading.

For personal use

This is an informational site for a personal Desktop application. The owner controls the application, backup account, and separately saved recovery key.
